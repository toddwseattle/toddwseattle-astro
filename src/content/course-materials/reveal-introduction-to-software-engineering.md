## Course Overview

Course Focus: Building software reliably and predictably in teams.

- Coursework: Programming, class discussions, and group projects
- Interaction with guest speakers and outside clients
- Main Deliverable: Your Team
- Two Projects
  - Startup Project based on your own ideas
  - Client Project where multiple teams work with a client on their product concept using AI and harness development.
- Self paced labs for skill building
- Evaluation: Based on a series of factors in group project
  - Team feedback
  - Quality of Deliverable
